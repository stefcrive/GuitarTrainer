# Instrument samples

FluidR3 General MIDI samples by Frank Wen, packaged by Benjamin Gleitzman in
[midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts).
Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The acoustic nylon guitar, steel guitar, grand piano and electric piano MP3 banks
are redistributed as JSON (wrapper removed; audio unchanged).
